# Removed

Every item withdrawn from the OpenFilm Community, newest first. A withdrawn item is in [revoked.json](revoked.json),
built into `index.json` (assets: [assets/revoked.json](assets/revoked.json)), and every OpenFilm disables it wherever it is
installed. Entries are never deleted; an appeal's outcome is added to its line.

Reasons: **malware** (harms the person or their computer), **impersonation** (passes itself off as someone else's),
**rights** (shared without the right to), **broken** (stopped working and was not fixed), **policy** (breaks the
[developer policy](POLICIES/developer-policy.md) or the [community guidelines](POLICIES/community-guidelines.md)). See
[takedown and appeals](POLICIES/takedown-and-appeals.md).
