# Licensing

One licence rule for code, one for assets, so everyone can use what is listed without reading fine print.

## Code: open source

Skills, workbenches and connectors are open source.

- The repository has a licence approved by the Open Source Initiative, in `LICENSE` at its root. MIT is a good default;
  OpenFilm's own skills use it.
- Everything that runs is covered by it and is in the repository as source.
- Third-party code you include keeps its own licence and notices, and that licence is open source too. Say what you
  included and where it came from in your README.
- A listed version stays available under its licence: the index pins its commit.

## Assets: CC0

Templates, text styles, stickers, effects, transitions, filters, sounds and music are shared under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) only: anyone may use them for anything, without credit.

- Share only your own work, or work already published under CC0 whose source you can show.
- Keep out third-party samples, music, fonts, trademarks and identifiable people unless you can document every
  permission.
- Do not register an asset with a system that claims matching uploads.

Assets are submitted to [assets/](../assets) in this repository; its [CONTRIBUTING.md](../assets/CONTRIBUTING.md) says
how, and what evidence a maintainer needs.

## AI-generated work

Anything made in whole or in part with a generative tool says so: in the pull request, and in the item's source
record. Undisclosed generated work is withdrawn. The library says which sources it accepts at a given time; at launch it
accepts no generated assets.

Code written with an AI assistant is fine. You are its author, and it is reviewed like any other code.

## Adaptations

An item based on another is clearly different from it, credits it, and follows its licence.

## Rights claims

If something listed is yours and was shared without your permission, email legal@openfilm.dev with the item's full
name, where your work was published, and the basis of your claim. See [takedown and appeals](takedown-and-appeals.md).
