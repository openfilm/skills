# Community guidelines

The OpenFilm Community is where people share what they make for OpenFilm: skills, workbenches, connectors and assets.
These guidelines are for everything shared and for how we treat each other in issues, pull requests and discussions.

## What can be shared

Share what helps people make films. Everything listed is free to use, and assets are free to use commercially.

Not allowed, in items, their covers, previews and descriptions:

- anything illegal where it is published or used, or that helps someone break the law;
- sexual content involving minors, in any form; sexual content of real people without their consent;
- content that attacks people for who they are (race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation,
  disability, age), or praises violence against them;
- harassment, threats, and anything that targets a private person;
- personal data of others: faces, voices, names, contact details, without their permission;
- work you have no right to share: someone else's footage, music, fonts, logos or characters
  ([licensing](licensing.md));
- deception: fake reviews or usage, names or covers made to look like someone else's, promises the item does not keep;
- malware, and anything the [developer policy](developer-policy.md) bans.

## How we treat each other

- Be kind and assume good faith. Most people are here to make something good.
- Criticize work, not people. Say what is wrong and, when you can, how to fix it.
- Keep discussions on topic. Report problems with an item through its report form, not in unrelated threads.
- No spam, self-promotion out of place, or asking people to boost an item.
- Respect maintainers' and reviewers' time: follow the templates, answer questions, keep pull requests small.

## Credit

Say who made what. Build on others' work only as its licence allows, credit them, and make your version clearly
different. An item that copies another with a new name is not listed.

## When something is wrong

Report an item with the [report form](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/report.yml), or privately by email to
support@openfilm.dev when the evidence is personal. Report a security problem privately ([SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md)).
Maintainers act as [takedown and appeals](takedown-and-appeals.md) says, and every removal is logged in
[REMOVED.md](../REMOVED.md).

Breaking these guidelines can mean a warning, an item hidden or withdrawn, or losing the right to publish.
